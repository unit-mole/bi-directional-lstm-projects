# Fairness, Privacy, and Limitations

This project is for educational and portfolio demonstration purposes only. It should not be used as the sole basis for hiring, rejection, promotion, compensation, immigration, legal, or employment decisions.

Resume–job models can reproduce historical bias, reward specific writing styles, overlook transferable skills, and disadvantage candidates whose experience is expressed differently. The system does not verify truthfulness, soft skills, work authorization, accommodations, job performance, or legal eligibility.

Do not upload real private resumes to a public demo. Production use would require representative data, subgroup fairness analysis, human review, explainability, security and access controls, data minimization, retention policies, candidate notice, audit trails, incident response, and legal compliance.
